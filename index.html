Shape the world

Everything here is clay. Sculpt: build, carve, groove, pull, slice, flatten, smooth, or draw coils of clay. Shapes: press clay into sphere, cone, cube, cylinder, torus or gem molds, lay bricks, then select, move, stretch, twist, flip, group or erase. Cast new molds solid or hollow (a thin shell) with the switch beside the colors. Paint: glaze spots, recolor whole shapes, or pick a color from the clay. Crystal, the last color, turns a shape or group into see-through glass; glaze or recolor it with any other color to stain it. Skins put photos on shapes: take one with the camera, choose one from your pictures, or snapshot what you see, then use the Skin tool or a selection’s Look page. Point the Skin tool at the land to lay a photo on the ground or a cliff. Fits wrap a photo across the front, all the way round (360°), onto each side, or straight through; the Photo arrows, turn and size buttons move the photo on a shape or a land skin. Billboards are framed windows on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Threads and Snapchat posts: add a link in Social links, press Place and point. A billboard sticks to the shape you put it on or stays fixed in space, resizes to any shape (16:9, 9:16, square, post), and plays when you click it or by itself when you walk up (muted). In VR a click opens it on the flat screen with a Back to VR button, since headsets can’t show web players inside VR. Phone link lets your phone send photos, 360° skies, sounds and social links: skies become scenes, and sounds go on shapes (touch or loop), behind a scene, or onto your tools. The panel has four sides: Create & Construct, Sounds & Images, Social links and Phone; Tab or the ⌄ button hides it. Tourist view (menu) is for walking round and exploring without tools. People who join your room are tourists unless you turn on Visitors can build in Play together. The world goes on in every direction, generated as you explore, and it saves on this device.

Places: mark up to four spots, each with your size (or the AR board’s scale), on the Places panel (the pin at the top, or the wrist panel’s Places tab). Start always brings you home at normal size. On a Quest, Endless Clay opens straight into the headset. Use “Switch to AR” or “Switch to VR” on the Room page of your wrist panel to move between your clay world and your own room. To play together, host or join a room before going in, since codes can’t be typed in the headset.
