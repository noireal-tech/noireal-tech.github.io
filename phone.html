Link to your headset

In Endless Clay, turn the control panel to Phone (on your wrist in VR) and turn the link on. Enter the code it shows, or scan its QR code. Once this app is installed you can also share photos, sounds and links to it from other apps.

Linked to your headset

Photos for skins

Photos land in the headset’s Skin photos. If shapes are selected there, they go straight on. They’re sent at the size the headset uses, so they arrive quickly.

360° skies

Use a 360° photo from a 360 camera or your camera’s photo-sphere mode. A panorama or an ordinary photo works too: it’s wrapped all the way round.

Standard skies are 2048 × 1024 and send fast. High quality looks sharper all round you in the headset, but is up to four times bigger to send.
